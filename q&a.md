# Spaceship X26 — Mission brief Q&A (quiz)

The PDF *Passenger Resource Management* describes the PRMS for Spaceship X26. Below are answers you would give if quizzed on that brief (not a formal multiple-choice key).

**What is Spaceship X26’s mission context?**  
Earth → Mars settlement mission. Passengers depend on **onboard resources**: sleeping pods, food stations, oxygen refills, medical bays, hygiene/fitness, etc.

**Who operates the system administratively?**  
Exactly **three Crew Leads** manage passengers and resources. They are the only roles with **exclusive administrative permissions** so the ecosystem stays controlled.

**What is the passenger “levels” rule?**  
**Higher levels inherit lower-level access**: Silver is the base; Gold adds more and keeps Silver; Platinum adds the top-tier facilities and **everything in Gold and Silver**.

**What does Silver (base tier) include (per brief)?**  
Food stations, sleeping pods, basic hygiene.

**What does Gold add?**  
Private cabins, advanced medical bay, **plus all Silver** access.

**What does Platinum add?**  
Luxury O₂ pods, VIP rec deck, **plus all Gold and Silver** (full ship access as described).

**What administrative powers do Crew Leads have?**  
Create/manage passenger profiles, **upgrade/downgrade** membership levels, **provision and decommission** ship resources, **monitor real-time activity** and usage/reporting views.

**What should passengers be able to do in the product?**  
**Resource discovery** — see facilities **allowed for their tier**. **Active usage** — start using an allowed resource with **real-time validation** of rules.

**What does Level 1 of the challenge emphasize?**  
**Crew Lead enforcement** (e.g. exactly three admins / safe limits), **resource definition** with **minimum required tier**, **passenger discovery** of filtered accessible resources, base inventory.

**What does Level 2 add?**  
**Permission validation** before use, **tier management** tools for Crew Leads, **audit logging** of resource interactions.

**What does Level 3 add?**  
**Personal history** for passengers (own consumption / trends), **aggregated reports** for Crew Leads (e.g. by level), **usage analytics** to spot high-demand resources (e.g. luxury oxygen) and shortages.

**What is the “mission success” idea in the summary?**  
Settlers get the resources they need; Crew Leads can **manage the ecosystem**; passengers get **transparent, reliable** access to life-support services.
